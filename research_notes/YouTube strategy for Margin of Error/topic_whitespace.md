# Topic white space: high-demand, under-covered cases with primary records (as of 06 NOV 2026)

Method note. About 18 web searches and fetches on 06 NOV 2026, plus the repo's own YouTube scan
(`docs/research/2026-11-02_youtube_scan.csv`, 1,045 videos from US/GB searches on 02 NOV 2026). That scan only covers the
search phrases it ran. Many cases below (Toronto, Iberia, Heathrow, Optus, CSB reports, Carmont, Philips) were **not
searched** in it, so "0 hits" means "not measured", not "no coverage". No Google Trends data could be pulled (gap).
Already made or on the slate, so excluded: Norfolk Southern East Palestine, Alaska 1282 (`four-missing-bolts`), the
transformer/GOES film. Strength tags: **strong** = primary record or several consistent reputable reports; **medium**
= one reputable secondary report; **weak** = thin sourcing, conflicting sources or unverified memory.

## Q1. Which 20 cases rank highest for a document-led forensic film, and what is the record behind each?

### Takeaway
The best openings for late 2026 are final reports that came out in 2025-2026 where the existing YouTube coverage is
pilot-reaction or news-cycle footage made *before* the report: the Potomac midair (NTSB AIR-26-02), Baltimore Key Bridge
(NTSB, Nov 2025), the Iberian blackout (ENTSO-E final, ~Mar 2026), Heathrow's North Hyde substation fire (NESO final,
Jul 2025) and Titan (USCG MBI Aug 2025 + NTSB Oct 2025). UPS 2976 has the deepest demand on the list but no final report
yet, so it is the next "final report slot". Grenfell and Post Office Horizon carry UK contempt-of-court timing risk.

### Inferences
- **Proposed ranking (demand x white space x record x dated decision):** 1 Potomac midair; 2 Key Bridge; 3 Heathrow North Hyde; 4 Titan (from the MBI record, against the pre-report myth); 5 Iberian blackout; 6 UPS 2976 (rises to #1 the day the NTSB final publishes; prepare now); 7 Optus Triple Zero (two outages, one set of ignored lessons); 8 Grenfell (window closes once charges are laid; check UK contempt rules); 9 Surfside (if NIST is out); 10 Post Office Horizon (wait for Volume 2; contempt check); 11 Toronto CRJ900 (if TSB final out); 12 Clairton (CSB); 13 Shell Monaca (CSB); 14 CrowdStrike; 15 Texas 2021; 16 Camp Fire; 17 Maui; 18 Carmont; 19 Philadelphia medevac; 20 Air India 171 (hold).
- **The missing angle, by case:** Potomac: existing films are pilot reactions and a 64-min pilot explainer; nobody has walked the route chart and the dated recommendations the FAA did not act on as documents. Key Bridge: existing films explain the wire; the edge is the 58 s to 10 s margin, the breaker mode and fuel pump practice as a chain of small margins, and the bridge-owner side. Titan: the top videos predate both official reports, so "what the 335-page MBI actually says" versus the 2023 consensus is open. Heathrow: a single dated fault (2018) plus two documented suppression findings (2022, 2024) is a near-perfect Act III, and it looks thinly covered on YouTube (not measured). Iberia: a neutral, physics-first voltage-control film is open and directly counters the partisan "renewables did it" framing; it suits the "both halves" rule. Optus: Bean 2024 recommendations vs. the Sept 2025 firewall change is a clean money-and-margin structure (outsourcing, risk classification).
- Cases whose record shows no corporate/regulatory decision yet (Toronto, Shell Monaca, Philadelphia) are weaker for Act III under the playbook rule "no dated decision, the case waits".
- Timeliness hooks in 2026-2027: Potomac first anniversary passed (29 JAN 2026) but the Army/FAA dispute is live (Oct 2026 video); UPS one-year mark just passed (04 NOV 2026) with the final pending; Grenfell charging decisions and 10th anniversary by 14 JUN 2027; Key Bridge rebuild and litigation (not checked); Iberia first anniversary passed (28 APR 2026).
