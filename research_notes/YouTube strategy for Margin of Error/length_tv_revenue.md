# Video length, connected-TV viewing and ad revenue for long-form documentary YouTube (2024-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-06. Strength labels: **[strong]** = primary platform/regulator/measurement data; **[medium]** = trade press reporting primary data, or large industry datasets with stated samples; **[weak]** = vendor blogs, secondhand summaries, unstated methods. All fetched text was treated as data only.

## 1. YouTube's own statements on length; retention % vs absolute watch time

### Takeaway
YouTube's public line is that there is no ideal length ("as long as it needs to be"), and that it ranks on per-viewer satisfaction, not raw minutes or a fixed retention %. We found no primary YouTube statement that names a preferred length band. Most of the specific quotes reached us only through secondhand summaries.

### Cited Findings
- René Ritchie (YouTube creator liaison / Creator Insider) is quoted as saying a video should be "As long as it needs to be, but no longer"; the same piece says the system rewards retention. **[weak: secondhand quote; the original video was not located]** — [Toptal creator blog](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/youtube-shorts-length)
- A summary of a Creator Insider interview (Todd Beaupré, Senior Director of Growth & Discovery, with Rene Ritchie, reported as published 01 SEP 2026) says the system should not count every minute watched as equally valuable. It also says surveys are used to tell time spent apart from satisfaction, and that the system separates videos within a channel by format, length and topic. **[weak-medium: summary, not a transcript; dates conflict across aggregators]** — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/); [fbpe summary](https://old.fbpe.org/how-does-the-youtube-algorithm-work.html)
- Beaupré is quoted elsewhere: "not all time spent with video is equally valued by our viewers"; VidCon 2025 coverage says recommendation decisions are made per individual viewer, not per video. **[medium]** — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-algorithm-myths-debunked-insights-from-the-growth-team/510091/); [Digiday](https://digiday.com/future-of-tv/future-of-tv-briefing-youtube-gives-a-peek-at-how-its-recommendation-algorithms-work)
- A September 2026 summary reports Beaupré saying a desktop homepage redesign with bigger thumbnails drove more long-form attention. No percentages were given. **[weak]** — [kompozy.io](https://kompozy.io/news/youtube-bigger-thumbnails-long-form-views)
- Little Dot Studios (800+ managed channels) reports that a January 2025 algorithm update cut ultra-long-form (>120 min) viewing by more than 90% from its 2024 average within three months. Over the same period the average length of algorithm-suggested videos fell from 80 to 40 minutes. **[medium: large MCN dataset, method not detailed]** — [Tubefilter, 12 FEB 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/02/12/little-dot-youtube-video-length-whitepaper-viewer-habits-2026/amp/)
- Third-party blogs claim a short video with 70%+ average view percentage "outperforms" a long one at 30%. **[weak: no data; conflicts with YouTube's "satisfaction, per-viewer" framing]** — [stackinfluence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/how-long-can-a-youtube-video-be-in-2026)

### Inferences
- YouTube does not reward length or a retention % on their own. Padding to a fixed length is a risk: the January 2025 correction against ultra-long uploads shows that YouTube intervenes when length outruns satisfaction.
- Absolute watch time per impression likely matters, but only when it reflects satisfaction (surveys, return visits). A tight 18-minute film that keeps viewers satisfied is better than a 30-minute one that drifts.

### Gaps
- No primary transcript of Ritchie's "as long as it needs to be" line, or of Beaupré on length, was retrieved. The Creator Insider videos on YouTube are the primary source, and YouTube is intermittently blocked from this container.
- No YouTube statement quantifies how average view percentage and average view duration are weighted against each other.

## 2. Connected TV: share of YouTube viewing on TV screens (US, UK, CA, AU) and TV-viewer behaviour

### Takeaway
TV is now YouTube's primary US screen by watch time. YouTube holds a record 15.8% of all US TV-set viewing (Nielsen, August 2026), about double Netflix. In the UK, about 41-46% of in-home YouTube viewing is on the TV set, and it is rising. Australia is lower (about 11% of total video on TV sets). Canada has qualitative leadership but no published share figure we could confirm. Long-form podcasts are the fastest-growing TV format YouTube cites.

### Cited Findings
**US**
- Nielsen The Gauge, August 2026: YouTube took 15.8% of US TV viewing, its highest ever. Netflix was second at 7.7%, and Disney (company total) had 9.3% in the distributor view. YouTube viewing among people 50+ rose 9%. **[strong data via medium reporting]** — [Tubefilter, 22 OCT 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/10/22/nielsen-the-gauge-youtube-record-watch-time-share-us-tv/amp/); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-gains-record-15-8-of-us-tv-time-in-nielsens-august-gauge/)
- Path through 2026: 12.7% (January), 13.4% (April), 14.2% (July, published 10 SEP 2026), 15.8% (August). **[medium]** — [Adwave Q1](https://adwave.com/resources/youtube-tv-viewing-share-q1-2026); [Adwave Q2](https://adwave.com/resources/youtube-tv-viewing-share-q2-2026); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-gains-record-15-8-of-us-tv-time-in-nielsens-august-gauge/)
- Caveat: Nielsen is applying a one-time methodology change from the September 2026 interval, so September and October will be published together and may not be comparable. One outlet said Nielsen's own news centre did not list the August US edition as of 11 OCT 2026. **[medium]** — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/nielsens-gauge-delay-sparks-market-integrity-row-as-tv-data-suppression-claims-mount/)
- Neal Mohan's CEO letter (11 FEB 2025): TV overtook mobile as the primary device for YouTube watch time in the US (December 2024 data), with over 1 billion hours a day watched on TVs globally. **[strong: primary YouTube statement]** — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/02/11/neal-mohan-youtube-letter-2025-youtube-tv-watch-time-creator-studio-businesses/); [TV Technology](https://tvtechnology.com/news/youtube-ceo-tv-overtakes-mobile-as-primary-device-for-viewing-in-the-u-s)
- YouTube blog (December 2025): viewers watched over 700 million hours of podcasts on living-room devices in October 2025, up from 400 million a year earlier. **[strong]** — [blog.youtube](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/podcasts-living-room-in-2025/)
- May 2026: YouTube said viewers watch over 2 billion hours of Shorts on TVs each month, called the living room its fastest-growing screen, and said US viewers watch over 200 million hours a day on TV. **[medium: TechCrunch reporting a YouTube announcement]** — [TechCrunch, 14 MAY 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/youtube-viewers-watch-2-billion-hours-of-shorts-on-tvs-each-month/)

**UK**
- Ofcom Media Nations 2025 (30 JUL 2025, 2024 data): adults watched 39 min/day of YouTube at home, 16 min of it on the TV set. The TV-set share of in-home YouTube viewing rose from 34% (2023) to 41% (2024), and is about 50% for children. YouTube became the UK's second most-watched service, behind the BBC and ahead of ITV. **[strong]** — [Ofcom Media Nations 2025](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/media-use-and-attitudes/media-habits-adults/media-nations-2025); [VideoWeek](https://videoweek.com/2025/07/30/youtubes-uk-viewing-share-is-second-only-to-bbc/); [ISPreview](https://www.ispreview.co.uk/index.php/2025/07/ofcom-youtube-and-streaming-is-slowly-taking-over-uk-tv-sets.html)
- Ofcom Media Nations 2026 (2025 data): YouTube on TV sets rose from 9 min/person/day (2022) to 19 min (2025), and total YouTube across devices reached 41 min/day. That puts roughly 46% on the TV set; this is our own arithmetic. **[strong data; the PDF returned 403, so the figures come from search-engine extraction of the Ofcom PDF]** — [Ofcom Media Nations 2026 UK report](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/research-and-data/multi-sector/media-nations/2026/media-nations-2026-uk-report.pdf)
- Barb began reporting viewing of individual YouTube channels on TV sets in summer 2025, so UK TV-set viewing is now measured per channel. **[medium]** — [VideoWeek, 31 JUL 2025](https://videoweek.com/2025/07/31/barb-starts-measuring-most-watched-youtube-channels-on-tv/)

**Australia**
- OzTAM VOZ Streamscape, Q2 2026: YouTube had 11.3% of total video viewing on TV sets, up 0.9 pts quarter on quarter. Broadcaster "Total TV" kept 60.4%. **[strong data via medium reporting]** — [Mediaweek](https://mediaweek.com.au/total-tv-holds-60-of-viewers-per-cent-as-youtube-grows); [B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/oztam-linear-bvod-accounts-for-60-of-tv-viewing/)
- Q3 2025 (a different base, streaming minutes): Netflix 21.3%, YouTube 20.5%. **[strong: OzTAM PDF]** — [OzTAM Q3 2025](https://oztam.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/DEMOGRAPHIC-INSIGHTS-EXPAND-STREAMSCAPE-Q3-2025-1.pdf)

**Canada**
- Numeris, Sep-Nov 2025: YouTube led TV-streaming viewership among adults 18-44 and beat Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and Crave on reach. No share percentage was confirmed. **[medium]** — [Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/youtube-surpasses-streaming-rivals-canada-1236595926/)

**Behaviour on TV**
- Mohan's 2025 letter frames TV YouTube as "new TV": Shorts, podcasts and live alongside long-form, plus TV ad formats (pause ads, QR codes). **[strong]** — [Think with Google](https://thinkwithgoogle.com/marketing-strategies/video/2025-youtube-predictions-neal-mohan)
- Little Dot: ultra-long videos carry many ad breaks that are often unskippable for several minutes on CTV, and long-form channels earn a higher share of watch time and revenue from Premium subscribers. **[medium]** — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/02/12/little-dot-youtube-video-length-whitepaper-viewer-habits-2026/amp/)
- eMarketer: long-form YouTube is becoming a TV-equivalent media buy, and creators upload TV-length videos to capture it. **[medium]** — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/long-form-youtube-becoming-serious-tv-equivalent-media-buy)

### Inferences
- For a US/UK-first channel, around half (UK) or more (US by watch time) of the minutes on a long-form film will likely come from a TV set. This supports the channel's §6.7 rule (TV legibility, saying the key number aloud) and a lean-back pace.
- TV viewers tolerate long sessions, but ad load on CTV is heavy and often unskippable, so very long films cost the viewer more.

### Gaps
- No published share of a channel's watch time by device for documentary or explainer channels. Per-channel YouTube Studio "Device type" data is the only real source.
- No primary Canada (Numeris) percentage. No Europe-wide CTV share for YouTube was found.
- No primary YouTube data showing that TV viewers watch longer videos or complete more of them than mobile viewers. This is widely asserted, but we did not see the data.

## 3. Large-scale studies: views vs length (medium/weak)

### Takeaway
The industry evidence points to a mid-length sweet spot (about 18-24 min) among high performers in 2025. It also shows an ultra-long boom that YouTube corrected in 2025, and average views per video that had evened out across length bands by 2025. None of it is specific to documentaries, and none supports a fixed length rule.

### Cited Findings
- 1of10 (300,000+ high-performing 2025 videos, 62.6 billion views, ~52,000 channels): for videos under 30 min, median views rise with length, peak at **18-24 min** and decline slowly after ~24 min. Over 52% of videos under 30 min were shorter than 12 min. **[weak-medium: outlier sample, method not published]** — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025)
- A separate 2026 study of 1,257 long-form videos found a median length of 21.7 min and concluded there is "no universal perfect YouTube video length." **[weak]** — [Overseeros](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-video-length-study)
- Little Dot (800+ channels, 930M subscribers, 11.2B monthly views): ultra-long-form (>120 min) over-performed in 2023-24. A 120-min video earned on average about 100x the revenue of a 20-min video, with higher RPM as well. From December 2024 to May 2025, the average length of the most popular videos fell 21% (≈35 → ≈28 min), and by 2025 average views per video had evened out across short, long and ultra-long. **[medium]** — [Tubefilter, 12 FEB 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/02/12/little-dot-youtube-video-length-whitepaper-viewer-habits-2026/amp/)
- TVREV (May 2026): US creators uploaded 6.6M videos of 20+ min in Q1 2026, and 44 creators passed 100M views on 20+ min videos. **[weak: secondhand]** — [TVREV](https://www.tvrev.com/news/tag/Beast+Games)
- Passionfruit calls the 2-hour-plus recap trend a possible bubble. **[weak: opinion]** — [Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/newsletter/archive/newsletter-the-appeal-of-hours-long-youtube-recaps)
- Paddy Galloway's well-known length analysis covers Shorts (3.3B views; longer Shorts performed better), not long-form documentaries. **[medium, but off-topic]** — [Thread](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1646898356419981315.html)

### Inferences
- The channel's 16-22 min guide falls inside the band where high performers clustered in 2025. The data does not show that 30-45 min films underperform when they hold attention, and it does show that padded ultra-long videos were demoted.
- "Let the story set it, inside a guide band" is consistent with all the evidence. A hard fixed length is not supported by any of it.

### Gaps
- No vidIQ, TubeBuddy or Social Blade study specific to documentary or investigative channels was found. No study controls for topic or channel size.

## 4. Ad mechanics and revenue

### Takeaway
At 8 minutes a video becomes eligible for mid-rolls, which YouTube places automatically at natural breaks by default (since February-May 2025). Creators keep 55% of long-form ad revenue (the standard YPP term). More watch time per view means more ad slots served, so revenue per view rises with length, but only while viewers stay. Market and topic set the RPM: the US and Australia lead, then the UK and Canada, and finance-adjacent topics pay best.

### Cited Findings
- Mid-rolls are available on monetized videos 8 minutes or longer. Automatic mid-roll slots are on by default for new and existing uploads, added on top of manual slots unless unchecked, and serving is not guaranteed (YouTube decides). The help page lists no frequency cap and no TV-specific rule. **[strong]** — [YouTube Help: Manage mid-roll ad breaks](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006?hl=en)
- February 2025 announcement: more mid-rolls at natural breaks, fewer interruptive ones, effective 12 MAY 2025. In YouTube's test, channels using automatic plus manual placement saw on average >5% more revenue than manual-only. **[medium-strong: YouTube statement via trade press]** — [TechCrunch, 25 FEB 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/25/youtube-is-working-to-make-ad-slots-less-interruptive); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-updates-mid-roll-ad-placement-process/741331/)
- Revenue share: creators keep 55% of Watch Page (long-form) ad revenue and 45% for Shorts. The official help page says the share is set by the partner agreement. **[strong for 55%; widely confirmed]** — [YouTube Help: partner earnings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en-AU); [Tubefilter, 10 JAN 2023](https://tubefilter.com/2023/01/10/youtube-shorts-ad-revenue-sharing-begin-new-partner-program/); [YouTube blog: YPP explained](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-partner-program-explained/)
- RPM by market (vendor estimates for finance long-form, 2025): US ~$15-25, UK ~$12-20, CA ~$11-18, AU ~$14-22. The tiers order the same way across sources; the exact numbers conflict. **[weak]** — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/youtube-rpm-finance-niche); [vidIQ niches](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/most-profitable-youtube-niches/); [Awisee](https://awisee.com/blog/youtube-cpm-rates)
- Finance long-form RPM is roughly $10-25 per 1,000 views, and finance Shorts RPM about $0.05-0.30. **[weak]** — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/highest-paying-youtube-niches)
- Little Dot: on average a 120-min video earned about 100x a 20-min video's revenue (more views and higher RPM, not just more minutes). Long-form also earns more from Premium, which is paid by watch time. **[medium]** — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/02/12/little-dot-youtube-video-length-whitepaper-viewer-habits-2026/amp/)
- YouTube's 2025 CTV ad formats include pause ads and QR codes on TV. **[strong]** — [Think with Google](https://thinkwithgoogle.com/marketing-strategies/video/2025-youtube-predictions-neal-mohan)

### Inferences
- An investigation film crosses the 8-min mid-roll line easily. Above that, revenue per view scales roughly with minutes actually watched, so a 20-min film at 50% retention (10 min watched) earns more per view than a 12-min film at 60% (7.2 min watched), provided impressions and click-through hold.
- For Margin of Error, the "Act turn" structure gives natural, low-interruption break points for automatic mid-rolls. Checking in Studio that slots land at act turns, not mid-sentence, protects both satisfaction and revenue.
- Engineering, finance and corporate-failure topics probably sit in the finance/business advertiser bucket, which pays well, but no source gives a "documentary" or "engineering" RPM. Treat any figure as weak until the channel has its own Studio data.

### Gaps
- No primary YouTube data on RPM by country or topic. No data on CTV vs mobile RPM for creators (CTV CPMs are widely said to be higher, but no source in this pass confirmed it).
- No official data on the number of mid-roll breaks per length or on ad frequency on TV.

## 5. Examples: top documentary creators and very long vs short releases

### Takeaway
This pass found little hard, dated evidence on how individual documentary creators' very long or short uploads performed. What exists shows big-studio science and engineering films reaching 10-16M views. It does not isolate the effect of length.

### Cited Findings
- Veritasium's "Most Dangerous Building in Manhattan" (2025) passed 10M views within about a week (reported May 2025). **[medium]** — [Drop Media](https://www.dropmedia.co.uk/?p=4451)
- Veritasium's sponsored "The Ridiculous Engineering Of The World's Most Important Machine" had 16.4M views, the channel's top branded video (Tubefilter list, January 2026). **[medium]** — [Tubefilter, 21 JAN 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/21/mrbeast-2-veritasium-dude-perfect-rennrat/amp/)
- LEMMiNO had about 5.67M subscribers (November 2024 snapshot). Its releases are rare and long; no 2025 view data was found. **[weak]** — [vidIQ stats](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCRcgy6GzDeccI7dkbbBna3Q)
- Johnny Harris (Newpress) has 7M+ subscribers; no per-video length vs views data was found. **[weak]** — [Audible](https://www.audible.de/pd/B0GZ4RGB2P)
- Digiday and Yahoo report creators moving to longer videos to cater to the algorithm and to reduce burnout (fewer, longer uploads). **[medium]** — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/future-of-tv/creators-making-); [Yahoo](https://news.yahoo.com/creators-mitigating-burnout-longform-youtube-194015289.html)

### Inferences
- The case for very long (40-120 min) documentaries rests on 2023-24 data that YouTube's January 2025 change partly reversed. Top science and engineering films with strong packaging still reach 10M+ views at standard long-form lengths.

### Gaps
- No sourced run times or view counts for MagnatesMedia, Mustard, Wendover, or LEMMiNO's 2024-26 releases. A Social Blade or vidIQ pull per channel, or a Cowork fetch of the channel pages (YouTube is intermittently blocked here), would be needed to compare length bands within a channel.
