# The money trail: what the record supports (2026-11-02)

The NTSB did not examine Boeing's finances and **could not assess whether adverse production pressure existed**
(AIR-25-04, finding 27). So Act III can show documented decisions and their documented effects; it cannot say a
financial decision caused the accident. Where we connect two records, the narration says it is our reading (PLAYBOOK
§5.3, §9.6).

## Not used (and why)
- "Piecework quotas" (handoff spec): no record found. Dropped.
- "The divestiture caused the accident": no record links them. The sale explains *why the fuselage, its plug and its
  rivets came from another company*, which is why rework crossed a company line (NTSB pp. 44–45). That is as far as
  the record goes.
- Buybacks → workforce cuts: no record links them. If shown, the narration says plainly that the NTSB did not
  examine finances, and that the two numbers sit side by side in Boeing's own filings, for the viewer to weigh.
- The DOT OIG audit (AV2025002, 9 Oct 2024): the PDF was not reachable from the session; cite through the NTSB's
  summary (p. 97) or fetch it later.
