# The Four Missing Bolts: treatment

Built from the record: NTSB AIR-25-04 (read in full), Boeing and Spirit SEC filings, the FAA's 24 January 2024
release. Facts carry their `claims.csv` ids (C01–C69); notes in `research/`.

## Logline
A 737 was built with a four-bolt safety margin on a 63-pound plug: a single one of its lower arrestor bolts would
have held it.
All four came out in the factory, and the piece of paper that would have put them back was never written. 108 days
later, at 14,830 feet, physics collected.

## The margin, in one line
The physical margin was generous (four bolts where one would hold, C14). The process margin, a removal record that
forces reinstallation and an inspection, was zero (C29, C37). The film is about the second margin: paperwork as
structure.

## Angles considered
1. **The missing paperwork** (chosen spine): the real retention device was a document. Accounting meets physics in
   the most literal way: a record that didn't exist let a part that did exist leave the airplane. Unique to us; no
   other film on this case is built on the final report.
2. **The biography of a part** (chosen as the structure device): the plug's life from Malaysia (March 2023) to
   Wichita, Renton, an open strap on a stand, a closed photo at 18:39, delivery, 154 cycles of creeping upward, and
   14,830 ft. A timeline bar runs through the whole film and returns at each act.
3. **Experience is a safety margin** (folded into Act III): the 737 line went from about 52 a month to under 10 and
   back to 38 (C42); experienced staff fell from about half to a quarter (C45); the one person who had opened these
   plugs was on leave (C31). Strong, but alone it becomes a labour story; inside angle 1 it explains *why the paper
   wasn't written*.

## What we can and cannot say (fairness, PLAYBOOK §9.6)
- Can: every finding as the NTSB's finding; Boeing's own executives' words, attributed; the FAA's words; numbers from
  Boeing's and Spirit's filings.
- Cannot: that production pressure, buybacks or the 2005 sale caused the accident. The NTSB **could not assess**
  whether adverse pressure existed (C47); the film says so in Act III, in those words. Buybacks (C56–C57) appear only
  as Boeing's own filed numbers set beside the workforce record, with the narration saying no investigator examined
  that link. Individuals are never named (the NTSB names none).
- The unidentified closer: the record says no one knows (C36). We keep that silence; it is the most powerful fact in
  the film and we never fill it.

## Feelings
Start: cold precision (a clock, an altitude, two empty seats). Darkest: the 18:39 photograph, and the quality sign-off
that "did not involve looking at the airplane." End: the fix (lanyards on the bolts so a missing one is visible), and
the unease that the margin that failed was never steel.

## Cold opens (3)
1. **The clock** (chosen): black-box-style FDR readout, 17:12:33, altitude ticking to 14,830, cabin altitude trace
   breaking upward; no music, a low hum; then one line: "Two seats beside the hole were empty." (C08)
2. **The dual split** (playbook §5.1): left, a blank Boeing removal-record field labelled NO RECORD FOUND (C29,
   reconstruction-labelled); right, the cabin pressure trace collapsing (C02). One sentence of fact.
3. **The photograph first**: 19 September 2023, 18:39, the text-message photo (C35), then the timeline bar racing
   108 days forward (C69) to 14,830 ft.
Kept as the open-loop payoff, not the opener: the photograph is stronger at minute nine than at second one.

## Signature moments
1. Four bolts, one at a time, the margin readout steady until none are left (C14).
2. The 18:39 photograph (C35), red circles on the empty positions, the timeline bar showing 108 days still to run.
3. The 154-cycle creep: a 63-lb plug weighing 25 at a low-g moment (C19), the gap growing by thousandths (C21).

## Visual world
The investigator's desk (brand §6): the NTSB report as the main exhibit (letterhead → finding → highlight), the
plug as a true-geometry cutaway built from the report's Figures 3–6 (redrawn, labelled ILLUSTRATION), a timeline bar
for the plug's life, FDR readouts for Act I (RECONSTRUCTION from the report's times and altitudes), Boeing and Spirit
filings as exhibits for Act III. No aircraft renders that could pass as footage.

**Rights to check before use:** NTSB-created figures and photos are US government works; Figure 18 and some others
are credited "Source: Boeing." Their use (short, credited, as news evidence) needs the owner's yes, or we redraw the
geometry and show the photo's content as a labelled reconstruction.

## Sound
Room tone and a 60 Hz hum, paper and stamp foley, quartz ticks on the clock, one synthesised cabin-altitude horn
(labelled synthesised in the manifest), wind noise at the separation. Silence before the 18:39 photograph.
