## Description

```
On 5 January 2024 a door plug left Alaska Airlines Flight 1282, a Boeing 737-9, over Portland, Oregon. The NTSB found that all four bolts meant to hold it down had been removed in Boeing's Renton factory and never put back, and that no record of the removal was ever written.

This film follows the investigators' record: how the plug is held, the 108 days between a factory photograph and the accident, the removal instruction, the workforce and the regulator, and the NTSB's probable cause. Findings are the NTSB's; our own reading is said as ours.
```
