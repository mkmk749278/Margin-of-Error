## Panel: top 10 fixes in order of impact

**1. Buyback juxtaposition.** This is the fairness and trust risk.
- **Line:** "In December twenty eighteen, Boeing's board approved up to twenty billion dollars of share buybacks. That year, Boeing spent nine billion dollars buying back its own stock."
- **Rewrite:** "In twenty eighteen, Boeing spent nine billion dollars buying back its own stock, and in December its board approved up to twenty billion more. [Add the suspension date from the filings, e.g. 'Boeing suspended buybacks in early twenty twenty.'] The workforce losses the investigation describes came after that."
- **Also:** cut "The two records sit side by side". Keep the N-T-S-B "could not assess" line.

**2. Non sequitur conclusion.**
- **Line:** "Read together, they suggest that the margin which ran out at Renton was not steel. It was a document no one wrote… and the experience that would have known to write it."
- **Rewrite:** Move it so it follows the workforce section, before the money section, and drop "Read together": "So the margin that ran out at Renton was not steel. It was a document no one wrote… and the experience that would have known to write it."

**3. Three versus four bolts.**
- **Line:** "And in the three bolt positions you can see… nothing."
- **Rewrite:** "Three of the four bolt positions are in view. In each, you can see… nothing."
- **Also:** align the thumbnail and say "three visible".

**4. "Weight" physics.**
- **Line:** "At the lowest point the analysis found, the sixty-three-pound plug weighed about twenty-five."
- **Rewrite:** "At the lowest point the analysis found, the force holding the sixty-three-pound plug down fell to about twenty-five pounds."
- **Also:** add before it: "In flight, cabin pressure presses the plug outward against its stops, and friction helps hold it there."

**5. "One would do" overstatement.**
- **Line:** "Four bolts, where one would do."
- **Rewrite:** "Four bolts, where a single lower bolt would have done."
- **Also:** at the close, use "Four bolts, where one lower bolt would have done."

**6. Unattributed painted-over rivets.**
- **Line:** "One repair was stamped as complete. Boeing's inspectors found the rivets had just been painted over."
- **Rewrite:** "One repair, by Spirit's team at Renton, was recorded as complete. According to the report, Boeing's inspectors found the damaged rivets had been painted over instead."
- **Also:** name the party only if the report does.

**7. Who opened the door.**
- **Line:** "The next morning, the door manager reported that the door was being opened by a mechanic."
- **Rewrite:** "The next morning, a Boeing door manager reported that a mechanic was opening the door. Who that was, the report does not say."
- **Also:** keep "Only Boeing's door team was allowed…" after it, so the tension is explicit rather than contradictory.

**8. Absolute finding on corrective actions, and the instruction commentary.**
- **Lines:** "For at least ten years, the investigators found, they did not work." and "It listed more exceptions than directions."
- **Rewrites:** "For at least ten years, the investigators found, those actions were not effective." and "The N - T - S - B found it listed more exceptions than directions."

**9. Inspector fairness.**
- **Line:** "Her sign-off, she told investigators, did not involve looking at the airplane."
- **Rewrite:** "Her sign-off, she told investigators, did not involve looking at the airplane. As the process was designed, it did not have to. She checked that the paperwork was complete."

**10. Spirit origin, recorder and cycles clarity.**
- **Lines and rewrites:**
  - "In two thousand and five, Boeing's operations in Wichita became Spirit AeroSystems" becomes "In two thousand and five, Boeing sold its Wichita operations to an investment firm, and they became Spirit AeroSystems."
  - "The voice recorder kept nothing from the flight" becomes "The voice recorder kept running after landing, and recorded over the flight."
  - "Across one hundred and fifty-four cycles" becomes "Across one hundred and fifty-four flights".

**Honourable mentions:**
- The strap line: "He lifted the plug back up with a strap, he said, and left it hanging there, partly open."
- The closing line: "The paperwork had none" could become "The process around it had none", to match the probable cause.

---

**Verdict:** Through the factory, the mechanism and the regulator sections it reads as a careful, well-attributed investigation. The buyback passage is the one place it slips into an opinion piece, and fixing that passage alone would close most of the gap in trust and fairness.
