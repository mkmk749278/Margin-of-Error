# Fresh-eyes picture review — The Four Missing Bolts, review cut r2 (720p)

Reviewer: independent session, did not build the film. Source: 17 contact sheets (one frame every 2 s, film time and
words heard above each frame) and `r2_narration.txt`, pulled from Drive `four-missing-bolts/review/r2_sheets`.
Checked against CLAUDE.md and PLAYBOOK §6, §9.4–9.6, §14.2. Small mono strips were read by zooming into the sheets;
where a strip could not be read at sheet resolution, that is said, and the finding is phrased as "verify".

**Overall:** the film's evidence spine is strong. The real NTSB pages, the 10-K and 8-K pages, and the fitting photos
are well chosen, highlighted to the exact line and on cue. The weak part is the reconstructed material between
exhibits: long stretches of small mono text on a near-empty void, several held 10–25 s, and a payoff photograph that
is a drawing. Act IV (the physics climax) is only about 1:44 long (11:18–13:02) and its key moment is visually the
faintest in the film.

Act boundaries seen: I 0:00–1:34 · II 1:34–3:50 · III 3:50–11:18 · IV 11:18–13:02 · V 13:02–16:14.

## BLOCKER

**B1 · 7:00–7:28 · The promised photograph is a drawing, and the honesty label is unreadable.**
What I see: at 1:10 the film promises "a photograph… It shows exactly what was missing. We will come to it in about
six minutes." At 7:14 what arrives is a cyan line drawing of the plug in a white photo-print mount, with red circles on
the bolt positions, while the narration says "It shows the plug, closed… In each of them you can see — nothing."
The only label is a tiny strip `RECONSTRUCTION · AFTER NTSB AIR-25-04 FIG. 18`, which is illegible on a phone.
Why it fails: (a) honesty — the photo-print styling plus "you can see" invites the viewer to take it as the
photograph; §9.4 requires the RECONSTRUCTION label, and at this size it effectively is not there. (b) The film's
biggest open loop pays off with its weakest image. The manifest says page 65 (the Boeing photo) was deliberately
excluded for rights, which is the right call; the picture still has to say so.
Fix: put a readable label on a card (not on the mount strip), e.g. `RECONSTRUCTION · THE PHOTOGRAPH IS NTSB AIR-25-04
FIG. 18 (BOEING) · REDRAWN HERE`, at least the size of the exhibit tags; drop the photo-print white border, or make it
clearly a drafting sheet; consider changing "you can see" to "it shows" or "the photograph shows". Or settle the
open rights question (treatment.md: "Rights decision on Boeing-sourced photographs") with the owner as a choice.

**B2 · 0:04–0:22 · Telemetry panel: no RECONSTRUCTION label, and an NTSB docket label on a panel we drew.**
What I see: source strip `FDR · NTSB DOCKET DCA24MA063 · FLIGHT DATA RECORDER · TABULAR DATA, ATTACHMENT 1`, right
corner unreadable. No `RECONSTRUCTION` seen. The clock and altitude interpolate between samples (17:12:22.6, 24.6, …).
Why it fails: §9.4 — telemetry drawn from a report's table is a RECONSTRUCTION; §6.5a — never an NTSB docket label on
something we drew. This is the first image of the film and the place a sceptical viewer checks.
Fix: strip reads `RECONSTRUCTION · FROM NTSB DOCKET DCA24MA063, FDR TABULAR DATA (ATT. 1)`. Verify the right-hand
corner text too.

**B3 · 1:10–1:20 · An empty "PHOTOGRAPH" box that reads as a placeholder, with a caption that names film time.**
What I see: a dark rectangle with the word `PHOTOGRAPH` in it, caption `19 SEP 2023 · ABOUT 18:39 · IN EVIDENCE AT
7:13`, over a 108-day timeline bar. About 10 s.
Why it fails: "polished only" — an empty box labelled PHOTOGRAPH is what a placeholder looks like; "IN EVIDENCE AT
7:13" refers to the film's own timecode, which breaks the investigator's-desk fiction. The same open-loop strip sits
in the top-right corner through all of Act II (`… PHOTOGRAPH · 19 SEP 2023 · ABOUT 18:39`), too small to read.
Fix: show something that is evidence: a sealed evidence envelope / exhibit tag `EXHIBIT · PHOTOGRAPH · 19 SEP 2023
18:39 · RENTON` with the bar, no film timecode. Keep the corner reminder only if it can be read.

## SHOULD FIX

**S1 · 2:58 and 3:04 · Bolt counter passes through "3 OF 4" while the words say one and two.**
At 2:58 ("One lower bolt") the readout says `3 OF 4`; at 3:04 ("The two upper bolts") it says `3 OF 4` again before
settling on `2 OF 4`. A count of bolts is discrete; interpolating it makes the screen contradict the narration. Step
it (or crossfade), don't count through intermediate values.

**S2 · 5:16–5:28 · "Just painted over" arrives about 4 s late.**
5:24–5:26 the words are "Boeing's quality inspectors recorded that they had not been replaced"; the card still shows
only `RECORDED: REPLACED` until 5:28. Bring the second line in on "quality inspectors". The card is also ~20 s of a
mostly empty panel with tiny type (5:16–5:36): enlarge the two lines and lose the empty area.

**S3 · 12:38–12:56 · The climax is the faintest image in the film.**
"The pins rode up off the pads. The upper fittings struck their rollers and split… up, out — and back." The plug is
the same small schematic, and at 12:54 it is a near-transparent ghost; the state tags (`PINS OFF THE PADS`, `SPLIT BY
THE ROLLERS`, `TWISTED`, `UP · OUT · AFT`) are small red mono. This is the reading that crosses the threshold and it
should be the most legible, largest move of Act IV: push in on the upper guide fitting, show the split (Fig. 12 is
already in the manifest), then pull out for the exit. Act IV as a whole is only ~1:44; there is room.

**S4 · 12:06–12:16 · "Rise toward the clearance threshold 0% → 94%" readout needs ILLUSTRATIVE VALUES.**
The flight counter (154, NTSB) is sourced; the percentage is not stated anywhere in the narration and looks modelled.
The bottom-right strip says ILLUSTRATIVE / NOT TO SCALE in tiny type; the readout itself needs the label (§9.4,
§14.2 "every readout its claim id or ILLUSTRATIVE").

**S5 · 12:18–12:26 · "Parallel lines of dust, dirt and petroleum jelly" is a flat drawing.**
~10 s of thin lines on a grey band, label unreadable. If the NTSB report has the photograph of the witness marks on
the seal, use it (public domain, NTSB-created); otherwise make the drawing larger and mark it RECONSTRUCTION legibly.

**S6 · Long static or near-empty stretches (nothing static over 2.5 s; a change about every 4 s).**
- 1:46–1:54 plug schematic held with no change (~8 s) over "a lighter, simpler panel".
- 3:54–4:10 "THE LIFE OF A PART": title top-left, everything else empty for ~16 s; the timeline bar is tiny.
- 4:34–4:58 same plug schematic ~25 s with small text additions.
- 6:04–6:20 two small cards ("ABOUT 25 YEARS", "NONE HAD EVER OPENED A PLUG") on an empty frame, ~16 s.
- 7:00–7:12 text-message mock: grey empty bubbles, unreadable header, ~12 s.
- 8:58–9:16 five inspector icons and the 68 / 0 counters in the top-left third of the frame; rest empty.
- 9:18–9:20 empty chart frame before any bar appears.
- 10:04–10:08 experience bars in a thin top band.
- 11:00–11:16 Finding 27 page held ~16 s; only the bottom tag changes.
- 13:16–13:40 probable-cause page ~24 s (the highlight moves; acceptable, but could push in on the contributing line).
- 14:40–15:06 fix schematic ~26 s with a small text list growing at the right.
- 15:08–15:28 "THE FIX IS FOR ONE PLUG" + one quote card on an empty void, ~20 s.
- 16:02–16:14 outro card ~12 s (fine for end screens, but the sources line is unreadable).
Most of these are the same problem: content sized for a 4K monitor, laid out in one corner. Scale up and centre,
and add one move per ~4 s.

**S7 · Phone readability of mono strips and labels (§14.2).**
Exhibit tags, source strips, readout captions, the Act II corner strip and most `RECONSTRUCTION`/`ILLUSTRATION`
strips are illegible at sheet scale (~480 px wide, about a phone in portrait). Honesty labels in particular must be
readable. Suggest a minimum mono size for labels that carry meaning (honesty labels, claim captions) roughly 2× the
current strip size, on a card.

**S8 · 8:36–8:46 · Counter card covers the highlighted line.**
The `11` / `16` card sits on top of "listed more exceptions about when the removal process was not needed than
guidance…", the very line the narration is reading. Move the card off the page or into the margin.

**S9 · 9:36–9:38 · Chart annotations overprint each other.**
Two amber annotations ("2018–19 · two fatal 737 Max accidents…" and "the pandemic…") crossfade in the same place and
read as garbage for ~2 s. Sequence them or place them apart.

**S10 · 7:38 · "1 DAYS".** Pluralise ("1 DAY") or count in whole frames so it never shows. Unpolished.

**S11 · 13:52–14:02 · FAA press release is too small to read.**
The page fills about a third of the frame; the highlight "This won't be back to business as usual for Boeing"
appears only at 13:58. Push in to the quote on "the administrator said".

**S12 · 11:36–11:42 · "> 150 LB · two people" on screen; the narration says only "It took two people".**
Make sure `> 150 LB` has a claim row (it's not in the narration), or drop the number.

## NICE

- **N1 · 0:44** — the track map opens as a single dot ("PORTLAND INTL · PDX") on an empty panel for 2 s; start with the
  track already drawing. 0:48–0:54 the map then holds ~8 s with only the injury counter changing.
- **N2 · 0:56–1:02** — "NO AUDIO FROM THE ACCIDENT FLIGHT" card is small and centred-low; could be larger.
- **N3 · 4:26–4:32** — the 10-K highlight takes in "approximately 64% and 19%"; 19% is Airbus. Highlight only "64%"
  for "nearly two thirds". A stray "milling" fragment from the previous page shows at the top edge.
- **N4 · 5:56–6:02** — the exhibit tag overlaps document text at the top-left of the page.
- **N5 · 6:46 and 13:50** — transitions briefly overprint (quote card over timeline; 171 counter over the FAA page).
- **N6 · 12:28–12:36** — exemplar photos show absolute gaps (0.079 / 0.315 / 0.630 in); the narration says the change
  was "about half an inch". A small `Δ ≈ 0.55 IN` between the first and last would tie the number to the words.
- **N7 · 10:56–10:58** — the "buyback · 0 results" search box floats on the bare void (§6.5a): put it on a mount
  whose strip says `OUR SEARCH · NTSB AIR-25-04 FULL TEXT`.
- **N8 · 14:34–14:38** — the 2005→2025 arc is a thin line and two labels; fine as a beat, could be bolder.
- **N9 · Fairness note (picture matches narration, flagging anyway):** at 14:08 the narration says Boeing's head of
  quality said "the company knew the move crew had closed the plug"; the page on screen reads "we believe the move
  crew; we know the move crew closed the plug". The screen is fairer than the words; consider matching the narration
  to the record's wording. Nothing else in the picture struck me as unfair to Boeing, Spirit, the FAA or any
  individual: allegations and findings are attributed, the buyback section is explicitly framed "not as a cause",
  and no victims' faces appear.

## Five strongest moments

1. **0:04–0:22 · The telemetry cold open.** Clock and altitude rolling, pressure falling off the cliff in red, CABIN
   ALT WARNING and MASTER CAUTION lighting exactly on the words. Clinical and gripping (fix the label, B2).
2. **3:12–3:18 · "4 WHERE 1 HOLDS"**, followed at 3:28–3:40 by the real fitting photographs with "no bolt contact
   damage" and the washer rings. Mechanism, margin and evidence in twenty seconds.
3. **6:22–6:30 · NO RECORD FOUND** stamped on the removal record, and its callback at 10:20–10:30 with "THE MARGIN
   THAT RAN OUT WAS NOT STEEL". The film's thesis as one image.
4. **10:56–10:58 · The search for "buyback" in the NTSB report: 0 results.** Fairness turned into a visual.
5. **15:48–16:00 · The ending:** the four bolts drawn in, then gone, `0 OF 4 BOLTS`. Understated and exact.
   (Close runner-up: 13:42–13:48, the 171 GROUNDED counter.)

## Five weakest stretches

1. **7:00–7:28 · The photograph payoff** (B1): grey text-bubble mock, then a drawing in a photo mount.
2. **1:10–1:20 · The empty PHOTOGRAPH box** with a film-timecode caption (B3).
3. **12:18–12:56 · The cascade climax:** flat dust lines, then a ghosted, small schematic for the separation (S3, S5).
4. **14:40–15:28 · The fix and "THE FIX IS FOR ONE PLUG":** ~46 s of small text on a static schematic and then on the void (S6).
5. **3:54–4:10 and 6:04–6:20 · "The life of a part" and the door-team cards:** corner text on an empty frame (S6).
