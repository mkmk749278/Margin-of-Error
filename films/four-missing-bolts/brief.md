## Records to collect first (PLAYBOOK §9.2)
1. NTSB final report (DCA24MA063), the board meeting abstract, findings and probable cause verbatim.
2. NTSB docket: Systems/Structures group reports, the factory-records exhibits, interview summaries, photos.
3. NTSB investigative hearing transcript, 6–7 August 2024.
4. NTSB preliminary report (February 2024).
5. FAA: the emergency airworthiness directive grounding the 737-9s, the FAA's audit of Boeing/Spirit production, any
   enforcement or production-cap letters.
6. Boeing and Spirit SEC filings: 10-K risk factors and the reacquisition 8-K/merger filings; the 2005 divestiture.
7. Congressional hearing records (Senate Commerce, 2024) for testimony on quality and production pressure.
8. Court records only as allegations (any securities class action), with status and date.
