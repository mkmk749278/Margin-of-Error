The fifteenth of July, twenty twenty-two. On its eightieth dive, the Titan submersible's hull monitor recorded a burst of sound, and a sudden shift in strain.

Eleven months later, on the way down to the Titanic, the hull failed. Stockton Rush, Paul-Henri Nargeolet, Shahzada Dawood, Suleman Dawood and Hamish Harding died.

Instead of inspecting the hull, OceanGate relied on this monitor to warn it when the hull was weakening.

It is often said the warning worked, and was ignored. The record is more exact. Three of the eight acoustic sensors were dead. Its alert criteria had been set on hulls built a different way. And the strain was read against time.

After the implosion, the N - T - S - B, America's accident investigator, plotted the same numbers against depth. We will draw that chart with them, from the reports and the sworn testimony.
