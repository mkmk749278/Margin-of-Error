## Logline
Titan's hull delaminated on its eightieth dive. Some of its own sensors recorded it; the way the readings were
plotted hid it; and the decisions that followed, each dated in the record, sent it back down.

## The margin, in one line
**Nobody ever measured it.** The design aimed at 6,000 metres with a 2.25 factor; scale models built the new way
imploded shallower than 3,000; the hull that carried people was the only one ever built that way, proof-held at 4,200
metres for twenty minutes, and never tested to failure (C45–C47). Then it was damaged, and still nobody measured it.

## Our edge (revised after the review)
Other films have told the hull's story in words (Scott Manley's 2024 film among them). **None draws the hull's own
dive data on screen: we re-plot them the way the NTSB did, run the hindsight test in the open (what OceanGate could
have seen, and when), and date every decision against the dive ledger, from the two final reports, the NTSB docket and
the sworn testimony.**

## Signature moments in full
1. **The unmeasured margin (3:45).** A vertical depth gauge, metres with feet and psi beside. On their words:
   `6,000 M × 2.25 · DESIGN GOAL` (dashed); `≈7,500 M · ANALYSIS, PERFECT GEOMETRY` (dashed, fades);
   `< 3,000 M · SCALE MODELS, SAME METHOD: IMPLODED` (red tick); `4,200 M · PROOF HOLD 20 MIN · 01 MAR 2021 · DOME LIMIT`;
   `3,840 M · WORKING DEPTH · 240-MIN HOLDS`. The cylinder's own strength line never draws: a bracket labelled
   `NEVER TESTED TO FAILURE · NTSB` where it should be.
2. **The re-plot and the hindsight test (9:45–11:45).** Six dives' group-4 hoop traces digitised from the NTSB Materials
   report figs. 28–31 (`DIGITISED FROM NTSB FIGURES · RECONSTRUCTION`). Against time: six humps. The axis label morphs
   `TIME` → `DEPTH (M)`; each trace re-flows. 75 and 80 settle on one line; 81, 82, 83 land a beat later and bend in
   the top 500 m. The slopes land one by one as said. Then the ledger dates slide in under the plot (`19 JUL · 22 JUL ·
   23 JUL`), and the narration runs the hindsight test, labelled `OUR READING`.
3. **"Definitely Stockton." (8:30).** The transcript page in desk pose, the question highlighted as read, the two words
   alone on a held frame for one second; introduced as "Phil Brooks testified". The narrator reads it as a quotation.
