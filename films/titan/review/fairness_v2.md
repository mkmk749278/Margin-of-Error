## Proceedings and law, as of 16 NOV 2026
- **Criminal:** none anywhere. The board says a Justice Department determination "will not occur in this instance due
  to the death of Mr. Rush" (ROI 6.5). Canada's RCMP announced an examination in JUN 2023; no outcome or charge found.
- **Civil (US):** the Nargeolet estate's wrongful-death suit, filed AUG 2024 in King County Superior Court, Washington,
  against OceanGate, Rush's estate and others (NPR/AP). In JUL 2025 the Rush estate sought dismissal on venue (Law360).
  Secondary sources up to mid-2026 report no ruling and no settlement. **Treat it as pending, and unverified:** read the
  King County docket before the film, description or any reply mentions it. The narration does not mention it, which is fine.
- **UK:** no inquest found; no arrest or charge. The Contempt of Court Act 1981 strict-liability rule covers UK
  proceedings only, so it is **not engaged**. Re-check before release.
- **Defamation:** Rush is dead. Neither England and Wales nor US law generally allows a defamation claim for the dead,
  so the test for him is respect and fair attribution. OceanGate "has been wound down" (its spokesperson, AP, 05 AUG
  2025); under the Defamation Act 2013 s.1(2) a trading company must show serious financial loss. **The real exposure
  is to living people**: Phil Brooks (named), and the unnamed but identifiable 2018 director of marine operations, first
  director of engineering, contractor, and directors of integration and of administration. Every line about them must be
  a fair and accurate report of the sworn record, with the context that changes meaning kept in. That is what the
  selective-quotation fixes below are for.
- **A third official report exists.** Canada's Transportation Safety Board released M23A0169 on 17 JUN 2026, with six
  recommendations. Two outlets quote it finding that defects and reduced compressive strength "likely led the cylinder to
  fail progressively, due to damage accumulating during each dive cycle", and that Titan "did not receive any oversight
  from TC" (Transport Canada; Global News, 17 JUN 2026). This contradicts L188 and makes L212's "neither report" ambiguous.
  The primary must come through the owner's Cowork route (TSB site blocked here).
