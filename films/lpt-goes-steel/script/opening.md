## Fairness notes (for the fairness pass at max before recording)
- "Only one mill in America makes the steel for transformer cores": the company and DOE both say sole producer (C10).
  It does not say the US uses its steel for most cores: the next sentences say most core steel is imported (C93).
- "recorded what transformer makers had told it": Commerce introduces the width point as "Another concern expressed by
  domestic transformer manufacturers" (C16). Never "found" for the width.
- "made an announcement about those cores": deliberately neutral; the words of the announcement come in Act III, as
  USTR wrote them (Mexico only, "in light of" a monitoring regime).
- "What followed took almost five years": 15 OCT 2020 to 18 AUG 2025 is 4 years 10 months (C92). It points to the
  calendar, which holds every federal action in the window, not to inaction.
- The DOE official's clip names no company and blames no one; it is used for what he says: there is one manufacturer,
  and the department works with it.
