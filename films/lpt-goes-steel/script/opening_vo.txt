Only one mill in America makes the steel for transformer cores. Its widest coil is nine hundred and twenty millimeters.

In twenty twenty, a Commerce Department report recorded what transformer makers had told it: larger cores need steel wider than nine hundred and thirty-two.

By the Energy Department's estimate, ninety percent of America's electricity passes through large power transformers. Most of their core steel is imported.

That report found the dependence on imported cores threatened national security. Three weeks later, the U - S Trade Representative made an announcement about those cores. What followed took almost five years.

We read the report, the Energy Department's studies, and the mill's own datasheets.
