## Verdict

**Not ready for the owner as written. Ready once the FIX rows are applied.** Nothing in the film accuses anyone of
wrongdoing. It has no guilt word ("fraud", "cover-up", "criminal", "negligent"), no "loophole" in our voice, no party
name and no partisan vocabulary. It never speaks as an outsider, sneers at a country or gives a motive without a
document. **UK contempt: nothing to stop for.** The film refers to no arrest, no charge and no active proceedings; the
BIS docket is an administrative rulemaking. The Act III spine (the 02 NOV 2020 release, the 05 NOV 2020 USTR
statement, CRS's "no other action", the calendar) is documentary. The calendar's content gives all three
administrations the same treatment.

Five things do not pass, most serious first:
1. **The heat lines say more against Cleveland-Cliffs' steel than its own datasheets allow.** As spoken, they mean
   "the US mill's steel cannot serve wound cores". The company sells non-refined grades it calls "most suitable for
   wound transfomer cores utilizing a stress-relief anneal". Only its laser-refined grades lose their treatment in the
   anneal. A fair-minded company lawyer would call these lines inaccurate. A product claim is also the realistic route
   to "serious financial loss" for a trading company under the Defamation Act 2013 (rows 1–5).
2. **One allegation is heard without anyone attached to it.** The narration never states "the circumvention claim"
   or says who made it. Viewers piece it together from our own juxtaposition, so it lands in our voice against
   Canadian and Mexican core makers. CRS names who argued it (rows 6–8).
3. **Three premises in our voice make the tariff the yardstick:** "what followed", "What did those years cost?" and
   "Why it took that long". The brief's case sheet says the record cannot show whether a tariff would have helped, and
   promises "The film says so". The narration never says it. Tariffs split the US audience by party (§4.1b) (rows 8,
   13, 14).
4. **The docket exchange hides both parties' stakes and the order of the filings.** Shown whole, the filing pages
   would also display charges we do not repeat (rows 9–11).
5. **The 40-year line is our reading, but nothing on air labels it as ours** (row 17).

**Count: 32 FIX (13 high, 7 medium, 12 low) and 3 OWNER'S CALL (rows 14, 16, 19).** 24 further lines were checked and
pass (rows 36–59).
