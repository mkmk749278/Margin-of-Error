## New facts to add as claims rows (after the fact-check's edits)
- N1 the fire was at the L1 bearing (RIR p. 104: "a fire on the L1 bearing of the hopper car").
- N2 the HBD came "shortly after" the cameras (p. 104).
- N3 an HBD is "a pair of upward-facing infrared cameras" (p. 4 n. 11); at Salem it read "the inner edge of the bearing cup" (p. 104).
- N4 "a bearing can go from below an alarm temperature to seized … in less than a minute" (p. 111).
- N5 overheated bearings come from increased friction (p. 4 n. 11).
- N6 "A fire ignited during the derailment" (p. x).
- N7 the TRRA yard at Madison, Illinois; TRRA co-owned by several Class I railroads including NS (p. 3 n.).
- N8 a bearing failure severe enough to derail a train "tends to destroy the evidence of its underlying cause" (p. 111).
- N9 four VCM cars released material from pressure relief devices (p. x).
- N11 finding 14, the consist delay.
- N12 polymerization of VCM is exothermic (RIR §1.8.1).
- N13 the incident commander was the East Palestine Fire Department's deputy chief (p. 9); a volunteer department (finding 11).
- N14 the 06 FEB meeting: 60–100 people including the governors of Ohio and Pennsylvania (p. 27).
- N15 speed 19–43 mph between 20:13:00 and 20:52:30 (event recorder CSV, OUR CALCULATION).
- N16 the AAR witness's title (p. 106: "the AAR Senior Vice President of Safety and Operations").
- N17 the report was adopted on 25 JUN 2024 (report cover).
- N18 operating ratio = "Ratio of railway operating expenses to railway operating revenues" (NS 10-K).
- N19 the fund's three payment categories: households, businesses, voluntary personal injury (Doc. 1053).
