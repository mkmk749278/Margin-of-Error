## Found in the checking that the review didn't have
- NS's operating rule (docket item 68): a non-critical message means slow to not less than 8 mph, then stop for
  inspection once the rear clears; a critical alarm means stop at once (C78, C79). The 170 line *is* the stop line.
- Before the accident NS's immediate-stop line was 200°F; after, 170°F (C77, identified by C74, consistent with p. 105).
- The federal consent decree had not been entered as of 09 MAR 2026; residents were allowed to intervene; the judge:
  "this is not a rubber stamp court" (C96–C98). The class settlement stands: appeals dismissed, certiorari denied,
  motions to reopen denied on 01 MAY 2026 (C99–C101).
- All five buyback years now quoted from the 10-Ks (C50, C51, C102–C104); total $12,819M, our calculation.
