# Review of the strategy paper "Forensic Industrial Autopsies" (received 2026-11-02)

The owner shared a 14-page strategy paper for the channel (not kept in the repo). This file records what we adopt,
what we check before use, and what we reject, with the reason. Nothing in the paper counts as a fact until a
`claims.csv` row confirms it (PLAYBOOK §9). Points marked **memory** are what this session remembers without a source:
reasons to check, never facts.

## 1. Agrees with our research (confirmed direction)
- **The position:** finance channels (ColdFusion, Company Man, Modern MBA) have no physics; engineering channels (Real
  Engineering, Practical Engineering) leave out the boardroom; macro channels (Wendover, PolyMatter) never show the
  document. Same finding as our YouTube scan (`2026-11-02_landscape.md` §1).
- **Defeating "AI slop" with primary sources:** real docket scans, code-rendered data, a clinical narrator. Same as
  our edge statement.

## 2. Adopt (added to the playbook)
1. **Case-sheet columns.** Every case gets four lines before research starts: the corporate decision, the physical
   failure mode, the "smoking gun" primary document, and the target length. Clear, and it forces the Act III link early.
2. **The record in the first frame.** The 0:00–0:06 "dual split" idea (a filing line on one side, the sensor trace on
   the other, no music, only a low hum and a mechanical click) is a strong cold-open option. It goes on the list of 3+
   cold opens per film, built only from sourced exhibits.
3. **Metadata anchors.** Titles and descriptions use the institutions and record ids (NTSB DCA24MA063, SEC Form 10-K,
   FRA, FAA), never tabloid words. This matches YouTube's documented relevance signals (title, description,
   captions).
4. **A "concealed warning" beat inside Act III** when the record holds one: the whistleblower memo, the ignored
   non-conformance report, the inspection finding that was overruled. Only from public records (dockets, hearing
   exhibits, court filings, committee reports).
5. **Act V cost reconciliation:** the documented saving set against the documented cost (fines, settlements,
   write-downs, compensation), every figure sourced and dated.
6. **Upload window default:** Thursday 14:00–16:00 US Eastern (19:00–21:00 UK). Low stakes, sensible. Studio data
   replaces it once we have it.
7. **Visual cadence:** a new focal point about every 3.5 s in the document acts (close to our 4 s).

## 3. Check before any use (the paper's case table)
The paper cites Wikipedia, Scribd, law-firm uploads and blogs beside the primary records, and several specifics
conflict with each other or with what we remember:
- **Alaska 1282:** "internal text messages confirming the plug was reinstalled without bolts." Memory: the NTSB said
  no record of the removal existed; use the report's exact wording on what the evidence shows.
- **East Palestine:** "a temperature spike exceeding 103°F above ambient." Memory: 103°F was a reading at an earlier
  detector, and the last detector read far higher; the paper mixes the readings. "30 seconds per car side" comes from
  a union interview: attribute it as the union's statement. "38-car pileup": check.
- **Titan:** "330 atmospheres / ~4,930 psi": these two figures don't match each other (4,930 psi is about 335 atm);
  take the depth and pressure from the MBI report.
- **Grenfell:** "saving ~£293,000" for the cladding switch: check the exact figure and what it covered in the
  Inquiry's own words.
- **Carillion / Royal Liverpool:** "£79M dividends in 2016", the beam cracks and "£100M+ retrofits": check each in
  HC 769 and the hospital records.
- **Deepwater Horizon:** "$1M/day rig rate", "$60M delay": check against the Commission report.
- **ERCOT:** "52 GW tripped": check the FERC-NERC figure and its definition.
- **PG&E Camp Fire:** the hook's age and the "diversion" framing: use the CPUC/Cal Fire findings and the company's
  own plea record; motive words only if a finding says so.
- **Key Bridge:** "deferred diagnostic clearing" and "port layover compression": no source given. Use the NTSB's
  findings only.
- **Didcot A:** high legal risk. If any UK criminal proceedings are active, reporting is restricted (UK contempt of
  court rules). Check the case status before any work; until then it is not on the slate.

## 4. Reject (and why)
- **Claims about how YouTube works that no source supports:** a "seed cohort of about 1,000 viewers", "regional CDN
  edge nodes" deciding who is shown a video, the transcript "mapping the video into an analytical taxonomy". These
  read as invention. We keep to what YouTube documents (PLAYBOOK §13.1).
- **"Premium CPMs of $14 to $28":** no source; we don't plan on any CPM.
- **Act I "the failure was mathematically impossible" and Act IV "material science is absolute":** false absolutes
  and drama; our voice states what the record shows.
- **"Unredacted corporate emails obtained during discovery"** as a routine source: only what is public in a court
  record or a committee publication.
- **Equations as decoration:** a crack-tip stress formula over a carbon-fibre hull is the wrong model (it is a metal
  fracture formula). An equation goes on screen only when the case's own report or a standard uses it, built up in
  motion and explained in the narration.
- **"No outro, cut straight to the end screen":** the owner's spec asks for an understated sign-off. Middle way: one
  clinical line over the final exhibit, the channel name, then the end screen; no "thanks for watching".
- **The paper's alternative act order** (paper trail before mechanism): the owner's five acts stay; the useful parts
  (concealed warning, cost reconciliation) fit inside them.
- **The illustrative cold-open figures** ("340 bar… certified to 600… twenty-two cents per share"): a template only;
  never spoken without sources.

**Owner decision (2026-11-02):** keep the approved slate; release each film when it is finished. Adopted points are in PLAYBOOK §4.3, §5.1, §5.3, §5.5, §8, §13.2, §13.4, §13.6 and §17.4.
